Micromouse 2025

Maze Navigation — Robofest Micromouse (2025)

Overview

A team-built autonomous Micromouse for Robofest 2025. I handled the hardware design, using Altium Designer to create the schematics and route the custom PCB. The ESP32-based board incorporates Time-of-Flight (ToF) sensors for distance measurement, alongside the necessary motor drivers and encoder feedback circuits used for the robot's navigation.
